DONUT SMP CLIENT MOD • FABRIC 1.21.X

Automatic Real-Time Economy Synchronization

The DonutLedger Fabric client mod automatically tracks your balance, /ah auction sales and purchases, /orders fulfillment, /sell server shop sales, and /pay player transfers on donutsmp.net and streams them directly to your personal ledger.

1

Download & Install Client Mod

Place DonutLedger.jar into your Minecraft .minecraft/mods/ directory and launch the game with Fabric 1.21.x.

Download DonutLedger.jar (Fabric 1.21.x)
2

Type /verify In-Game

Join the Donut SMP server (donutsmp.net) and type the command in chat:

/verify
Your unique API key is securely masked in chat and automatically copied to your Windows clipboard.
3

Sign In on Website

Click "Sign In" in the top right corner and paste your Minecraft username and API key from your clipboard.
